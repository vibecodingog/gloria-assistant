Privacy Policy

Gloria Assistant is a private, personal-use application used only by the account owners who authorize it. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Contacts data solely to help the account owner read, organize and respond to her own email, files and calendar.

Use and sharing

Data is used only to perform tasks the account owner requests. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
